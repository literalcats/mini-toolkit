Mini Toolkit

Five small Python tools, one app.

Compute age and days

Enter a birth date to see your exact age and how many days you've been alive.

Electricity calculator

Estimate energy use and cost from your appliances' wattage and daily usage.

Grade boundary checker

See which grade a score falls into and how far the next grade is. Boundaries are editable (minimum %).

Find the largest of three numbers

Type three numbers and the app picks the biggest (it also spots ties).

Secret number

I'm thinking of a number. Guess it, and I'll tell you higher or lower.
